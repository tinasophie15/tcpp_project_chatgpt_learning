Impact of ChatGPT on the Academic Writing Quality of Senior High School Students (Maghamil & Sieras, 2024)

1. General Description

This study explores ChatGPT's impact on Grade 12 students' writing quality using a pretest-posttest experimental design to address challenges in writing skills development.

2. What was done (Method)

54 Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) students were randomly assigned to control and experimental groups. The experimental group integrated ChatGPT into their writing process before being examined by three independent raters.

3. Main Results

While both groups started with poor/fair writing quality, the experimental group significantly improved to a "satisfactory" level in content, grammar, mechanics, and organization during the posttest, whereas the control group remained "fair".

4. Why this study is special/good for me

It gives our meta-analysis concrete experimental proof that ChatGPT acts as an efficient scaffolding tool to directly enhance the structural writing quality of students within a practical classroom setting.
