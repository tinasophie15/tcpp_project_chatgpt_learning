Impact of ChatGPT on the Academic Writing Quality of Senior High School Students

This page summarizes the study by Maghamil and Sieras (2024), which investigated whether integrating ChatGPT impacts the writing quality of Grade 12 students using a pretest-posttest experimental design.

The Study at a Glance

n = 54 students participated in the study from the Senior High School of Mindanao State University.
27 + 27 control group and ChatGPT-assisted group, randomly assigned.
Pre-Post to determine significant changes in the research groups resulting from the experimental intervention.
3 Raters independent examiners analyzed the outputs (the subject teacher, an English teacher from the institution and one not from the institution).

Study Overview

Design

Comparison between two Groups

One group wrote their assignments traditionally, while the other group integrated ChatGPT as an assistive tool to improve writing skills.

Task

Academic writing development

Students underwent pretests and posttests to examine changes in proficiency across content, grammar, mechanics, and organization.

Participants

Senior High School students

The participants were Grade 12 Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) tracks facing practical challenges in traditional writing skill development.

Assessment

Writing quality parameters

The researchers evaluated the outcomes based on four major domains: content, grammar, mechanics, and overall structural organization.

Main findings

Finding 1

Initial writing quality was low

During the pretest, writing performance was categorized as merely poor and fair for both student groups.

Finding 2

Significant boost with AI

The experimental group using ChatGPT improved their academic writing significantly to a "satisfactory" level during the posttest.

Finding 3

Control group remained stagnant

The control group, which engaged in traditional writing without ChatGPT, showed no significant progress and remained "fair".

Finding 4

Core metrics enhanced

The study concludes that large language models positively impact writing quality in terms of content, grammar, mechanics, and organization.

Why is this study relevant?

Because it probes the impact of ChatGPT on the academic writing quality of senior high school students.

This study is relevant because it provides concrete experimental data supporting the benefits of AI integration. It demonstrates a direct causal relationship between AI usage and academic improvement, showing that, with caution, ChatGPT can act as an efficient tool in secondary education.

Design perspective

Instructional foundation

For me, the study shows that, with the right implementation, integrating ChatGPT provides a concrete foundation for creating suitable instructional materials and teaching strategies for writing skill development.

Why is it a good example?

It is a good example because it measures ChatGPT's impact across specific parameters (content, grammar, mechanics, organization) with multiple independent raters, offering clear, quantitative data for our overall meta-analysis.

Limitations of the Study

  • The scope was restricted to Grade 12 TVL tracks in a specific school environment.
  • It did not include the assessment of other factors that may affect the students' writing quality, such as their personal motivation, writing experience, and learning styles.
  • It does not explain the Statistical Analysis used to get to the sample size.

Key Takeaway

Integrating ChatGPT in teaching writing skills can be beneficial and efficient when correctly implemented.

Maghamil & Sieras (2024) show that large language models improve the writing quality of Grade 12 students, proving that AI can be a powerful educational tool for developing writing proficiency.
